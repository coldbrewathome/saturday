# FamHop — YouTube Shorts, week of Jul 27 – Aug 2, 2026

Three 9:16 shorts, one design system, three different jobs. Sources:
`videos/famhop-short-bay`, `videos/famhop-short-count`, `videos/famhop-short-nyc`
(each is a HyperFrames project — `npx hyperframes render --quality high`).

Every fact on screen is read out of `public/data/<metro>/events.json`. If an event
is cancelled or re-timed before these publish, fix the frame — do not ship a wrong
time on a page we are asking people to trust.

**These are dated.** The Bay Area and NYC cuts expire Sun Aug 2, 2026; the counter
cut expires Sun Aug 2 too (its numbers are a one-week window). Re-cut weekly from
the same projects rather than leaving stale ones up.
