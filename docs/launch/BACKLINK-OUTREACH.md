# Backlink Outreach — Earned Links for famhop.com

_Compiled 2026-08-16. Per SEO-POLICY: earned links only, never bought/networked.
Goal: 3–5 relevant referring domains by end of Q3 (2026)._

Method: 6 web searches + ~20 targeted HTTP fetches (browser UA); "verified" URLs
returned HTTP 200 with matching page titles unless noted. No emails were scraped —
every contact is a publicly listed page URL. Libraries first: they already publish
community calendars and (several) maintain external "local links" pages, so a
family-weekend-events resource is a natural addition to their link inventory.

## Outreach angles (per org type)

- **Libraries with a links-out page (1–5):** "FamHop publishes dated, source-verified
  family events for your community (storytimes from your own calendar, parks, museums,
  festivals). Our {metro} weekend pages would fit your community-resources list."
  Link to a metro hub or a seasonal page (pumpkin patches once live, e.g.
  https://famhop.com/bay-area/event/lemos-farm-pumpkin-patch/).
- **Libraries without a links page (6–8, 10–12):** offer reciprocal value: "we list
  your system's own family programs on our site with links back to your calendar" —
  they already get referral traffic from famhop event pages (verified-source links
  on every event card point at the organizer's calendar).
- **Parks/community orgs (9, 5):** pitch the cross-listing of their free family
  events (Movies in the Park, park district programs) — same referral logic.

## Process notes

- One ask per org; personalize with the exact metro page; no link farms, no directories,
  no paid placements (policy).
- Measure weekly in GSC: referring-domains report; log new referrers in pulse-log.
- Re-verify BPL events page before sending (HTTP 500 on 2026-08-16).
- Follow-ups at 7 and 14 days; close the loop in the weekly pulse.
